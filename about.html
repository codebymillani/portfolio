My Story

Hey, I'm Millani Khabir

I'm a senior studying Computer Science at UCF, currently focused on landing a UX Research or Product Design internship this spring, and working toward a full-time UX Researcher & Product Designer role after graduation in May 2027.

My path here wasn't a straight line. I started college as a Computer Science major partly because my dad's a senior UX designer and I always figured I'd end up somewhere in that world eventually, but I wanted to see what else was out there first. I went through software engineering, digital media, data engineering, and data analytics before landing on UX research, where my actual interests, human behavior, why people do what they do, finally had somewhere to go.

I naturally gravitate toward structure and organization. Whether I'm running user interviews, synthesizing research into requirements, or managing a project timeline, I like creating systems that feel clear, efficient, and intentional. The CS background stays useful here too. I understand what's actually feasible to build, which makes it easier to work directly with engineers instead of just handing off a file and hoping.

Campus Involvement

Fashion Society at UCF: I lead the Website Design Committee as Web Design Chairman, coding and maintaining the organization's site and serving as liaison between web developers and other committees.

National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE): I'm an active member, participating in professional development conventions and community service initiatives as part of a network of Black engineers and technologists.

Royalty Dance Troupe: I'm a Captain on the team and help with choreography and performance coordination. I've danced since I was little, and it's always combined creativity, discipline, and attention to detail, which overlaps more with my research and design work than people usually expect.

Outside of Tech

Ten-plus years of competitive colorguard and now college dance means I'm still a performer at heart. I'm into fitness (yoga, pilates, the gym), and I play Valorant more than I probably should. I'm getting into sewing and design, and I have a real interest in modeling and acting, since performing has just always been part of who I am. I read a lot into astrology, cook new recipes when I have time, and I'm generally an optimist who's big on mindset and slow mornings. I'm also just naturally an organizer and analytical thinker, even outside of work, which probably explains a lot about why UX research fits.
