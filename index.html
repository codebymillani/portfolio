Hi, I'm Millani Khabir

UX Researcher & Product Designer

I've spent 10+ years performing, from competitive colorguard to college dance. My dad's a senior UX designer, and watching how much he loved his work stuck with me. I think I always knew I'd end up back there eventually. But I started college wanting to see what else was out there first, so I went in as a CS major and explored software engineering, digital media, data engineering, and data analytics along the way. Somewhere in that process I found myself: my actual love for human behavior, astrology, anthropology, psychology, and UX research was the one place all of that actually fit. I'm working toward a career as a UX Researcher & Product Designer.
