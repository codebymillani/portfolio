Hi, I'm Millani Khabir

I've spent 10+ years performing, from competitive colorguard to college dance. My dad's a senior UX designer, and watching how much he loved his work stuck with me. I think I always knew I'd end up back there eventually. I'm working toward a career as a UX Researcher & Product Designer.
